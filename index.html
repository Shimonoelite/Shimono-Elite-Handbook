3.1 What is Alkaline Water?3.1 什么是碱性水？

Alkaline water has a pH above 7.0. The Mizu V1 produces alkaline water through electrolysis — splitting water molecules to concentrate alkaline minerals on one side. This is fundamentally different from adding chemicals.碱性水的pH值高于7.0。Mizu V1 通过电解技术产生碱性水——将水分子分离，使碱性矿物质集中于一侧。这与添加化学物质的方式截然不同。

3.2 What is Hydrogen Water?3.2 什么是氢水？

Hydrogen water contains dissolved molecular hydrogen (H₂). The Mizu V1 produces water with up to 1,600 ppb of dissolved hydrogen — significantly higher than most competitors. Understanding the Customer了解顾客

Every Elite Circle agent must master one skill before any other: understanding the person in front of you. This chapter trains you to read customer motivations, recognise different buyer personalities, build genuine trust, and ask the kind of questions that uncover what a customer actually needs — before you ever talk about the Mizu V1.

Objective: Shift your mindset from “selling a machine” to “solving the customer's problem.” A roadshow visitor who feels understood is far more likely to become a customer who feels confident.

2.1 Why Do Customers Buy a Water Machine?

Customers rarely buy because of technology. They buy because they want to solve a problem in their life — and the Mizu V1 happens to be the tool that solves it.

Common buying motivations:

  • Better drinking water for the family
  • Concern about children's health
  • Elderly parents living at home
  • Convenience — no more carrying bottled water
  • Better tasting water
  • Long-term cost savings vs. bottled water
  • A better, more health-conscious lifestyle
  • Peace of mind
TRAINER NOTE People buy emotionally first and justify logically later. Your job in the first few minutes is to find the emotional reason — then your product knowledge gives them the logical justification to act on it.

2.2 Common Customer Types

No two customers are the same. Recognising which type you're speaking to — usually within the first minute — lets you adjust your approach instead of running the same script on everyone.

Family Buyer

Focus: Safety for children and parents.

Cares about: Water quality, convenience, maintenance, warranty.

Selling approach: Emphasise family benefits and daily usage — talk about mornings, school bottles, grandparents' medication water.

Health-Conscious Customer

Focus: Hydration, healthy lifestyle, exercise, nutrition.

Cares about: Hydrogen content, alkaline water, research, daily wellness.

Selling approach: Explain the product honestly using current scientific evidence. Never overstate what hydrogen water can do — credibility is your biggest asset with this customer.

Price-Sensitive Customer

Focus: Price.

Typical questions: “Why RM9,980?” “Why so expensive?”

Selling approach: Show long-term value. Break the price down to cost-per-day across the product's lifespan. Never argue about price — reframe around value instead.

Premium Customer

Focus: Quality, brand, technology, after-sales service.

Selling approach: Highlight product quality, certifications, warranty, and the long-term ownership experience. This customer is not looking for a discount — they are looking for confidence in the brand.

Analytical Customer

Focus: Facts, specifications, evidence.

Typical questions: “How does electrolysis work?” “What is ORP?” “What is hydrogen?” “Why 5 plates?”

Selling approach: Provide clear, accurate explanations. Never exaggerate a spec. If you don't know an answer, say so and follow up — guessing destroys trust permanently with this type.

Skeptical / Guarded Customer

Focus: Has likely been pitched before — by Kangen, Cuckoo, Coway agents, or MLM-style schemes — and is on guard against pressure tactics.

Typical signals: Crossed arms, short answers, “I'm just looking,” testing you with hard questions early.

Selling approach: Slow down. Don't chase. Give them space and let curiosity lead. Lower the stakes by offering a free water test or demo with zero obligation — this is often the only thing that gets a guarded customer to stay.

2.3 Understanding Customer Psychology

Beneath the surface, almost every customer is silently asking themselves the same five questions. If you can answer these — through your behaviour, not just your words — the sale becomes far easier:

  1. Can I trust you?
  2. Is this really useful for me?
  3. Is it worth RM9,980?
  4. Will my family actually benefit from this?
  5. What happens after I buy — am I on my own?
TRAINER NOTE Trust must be built before discussing price. A customer who trusts you but isn't sure about price will ask you for help finding a way to afford it. A customer who doesn't trust you will never ask — they'll just walk away.

2.4 Customer Buying Journey

Every customer moves through five stages. Trying to skip a stage — for example jumping straight to price before trust is built — is the most common reason a promising conversation falls apart.

2.5 Building Trust

Customers buy from people they trust — not from the agent with the best script. Trust is built through small, consistent behaviours, not a single clever line.

Always:

  • Smile and maintain genuine eye contact
  • Listen more than you speak
  • Ask questions before presenting
  • Never interrupt
  • Be honest when you're unsure of an answer
  • Never pressure a customer to decide on the spot

Four Trust-Building Habits Worth Practising

Give before you ask. Offering a genuinely free water test, a useful comparison, or an honest answer before asking for the sale makes customers naturally more open to listening to you in return.

Be consistent. If a customer agrees with a small statement early on (“Filtered water does taste better, right?”), they become more receptive to bigger statements later — because people like to stay consistent with what they've already agreed to.

Let proof speak for you. Real customer stories, the SIRIM/NSF/KKM certifications, and visible test results reassure a hesitant customer far more than your own claims do.

Find common ground. A customer who feels you genuinely relate to their situation — same neighbourhood, same concern for ageing parents, same frustration with bottled water — will trust you faster than one who feels “sold to.”

TRAINER NOTE These habits come from well-documented principles of trust and influence (reciprocity, consistency, social proof, and liking). Used honestly, they help a hesitant customer feel comfortable. Used manipulatively, they destroy trust the moment a customer notices. Always use them to help the customer see clearly — never to pressure them.

2.6 Discovery Questions

Presenting before you understand the customer is the single biggest mistake a new agent makes. Top-performing sales consultants spend most of the conversation asking, not pitching — and let the customer arrive at their own conclusion that the Mizu V1 fits their life.

A simple way to structure your questions is to move through four layers — starting broad, then narrowing toward the customer's own reason to act:

SituationUnderstand their current setup.“What water machine are you currently using?” “How many family members are at home?”
ProblemUncover a specific frustration.“Do you find yourself buying bottled water often?” “Has anyone in the family had concerns about tap water?”
ImplicationHelp them feel the real cost of the problem.“With young kids at home, does that water quality worry cross your mind often?” “How much do you think the family spends on bottled water a month?”
Need-PayoffLet them state the benefit themselves.“If your family had clean, great-tasting filtered water on tap every day, what would that change?”

  • What made you stop and have a look today?
  • What is most important to you when choosing a water machine?
  • Have you considered other brands before — Kangen, Cuckoo, Coway?
  • Which feature interests you the most?
  • Do you usually cook or prepare drinks at home with tap water?
TRAINER NOTE Keep questions open-ended and genuinely curious — never rapid-fire like an interrogation. The agent who asks better questions, and actually listens to the answers, consistently closes more sales than the agent who memorises the best pitch.

2.7 Active Listening

Listening for keywords lets you respond to what the customer actually means — not just what they said.

Customer says: “My kids always drink bottled water.”

Possible underlying concerns: water quality, safety, convenience.

Recommended response: “I understand — many parents feel the same way. Let me show you how Shimono Elite Mizu can give your family filtered water at home, every single day.”

A few more examples:

Customer says: “My parents are getting older and I worry about their health.”

Recommended response: “That's a very caring thought. A lot of our customers started exactly there — wanting better water for ageing parents at home. Can I share how families like yours are using the Mizu V1?”

Customer says: “I already have a filter at home.”

Recommended response: “That's great that you're already thinking about water quality. Mind if I ask what your current filter actually removes? That'll help me show you where Mizu V1 might add real value — or honestly, where it might not.”

2.8 Common Customer Concerns & How to Respond

Never dismiss a concern. Acknowledge it first, then respond calmly and honestly. A simple four- step method works for almost any objection:

  1. Acknowledge — show you heard them and that the concern is reasonable.
  2. Clarify — ask a short question to understand exactly what's behind the concern.
  3. Respond — answer honestly, using facts, not pressure.
  4. Confirm — check that your answer actually resolved their concern.

TRAINER NOTE Acknowledging a concern is not the same as agreeing the product is flawed — it simply tells the customer “I heard you, and I'm not going to brush this off.” That alone resolves a surprising number of objections before you even finish your answer.

2.9 Golden Rule

Customers don't buy a water ioniser.

They buy confidence, convenience, better daily habits, and peace of mind. The Mizu V1 is simply the tool that helps them get there.

2.5 建立信任

顾客购买的对象，是他们信任的人——而不是说辞最华丽的代理。信任是靠一连串细微而持续的言行累积起来的，而不是靠一句聪明的话。

务必做到：

  • 保持微笑与真诚的眼神接触
  • 多听，少说
  • 先提问，再介绍产品
  • 绝不打断顾客说话
  • 不确定答案时坦诚说明
  • 绝不施压要求顾客当场做决定

四个值得养成的建立信任习惯先付出，再请求。在提出成交之前，先提供真正免费的水质测试、有用的比较资讯，或一个诚实的答案，会让顾客更自然地愿意倾听您接下来要说的话。

保持言行一致。如果顾客在交谈初期同意了一个小观点（例如“过滤后的水确实比较好喝，对吧？”），他们之后会更容易接受较大的观点——因为人们倾向于让自己的言行保持一致。

让证据替您说话。真实的顾客案例、SIRIM/NSF/KKM 等认证，以及可见的测试结果，远比您自己的口头介绍更能安抚犹豫不决的顾客。

寻找共同点。如果顾客感受到您真正理解他们的处境——例如同一个社区、同样担心年迈的父母、

同样厌倦搬运瓶装水——他们建立信任的速度会比感觉“被推销”的顾客快得多。

TRAINER NOTE 以上习惯源自关于信任与说服力的经典原则（互惠、一致性、社会认同与好感）。诚实运用时，能帮助犹豫的顾客感到安心；若被用来操控顾客，一旦被察觉，信任将瞬间崩塌。请始终运用这些原则帮助顾客看清事实——而不是用来施压。

一个简单的提问架构，是按照四个层次逐步推进——从宽泛的问题开始，逐渐聚焦到顾客自己采取行动的理由：

层次目的示例问题

TRAINER NOTE 提问应保持开放式且真诚好奇，绝不能像审问一样连续追问。能提出更好问题，并真正倾听答案的代理，成交率始终高于只会背诵完美说辞的代理。

2.7 积极聆听

顾客说： “我的孩子一直都在喝瓶装水。”潜在的担忧：水质、安全性、方便性。

建议回应: “我明白，很多家长都有同样的想法。让我为您介绍一下 Shimono Elite Mizu，如何让您的家人每天在家就能喝到过滤水。”

更多示例：

顾客说: “我父母年纪大了，我很担心他们的健康。”

建议回应: “这真的是一份用心。很多顾客一开始也是这样——希望让家中年长的父母喝到更好的水。我可以分享一下，像您家这样的家庭是怎么使用 Mizu V1 的吗？”

顾客说: “我家里已经有滤水器了。”

建议回应: “您已经在关注水质问题，这很好。可以请问您目前的滤水器主要能过滤掉什么吗？这样我才能诚实地告诉您，Mizu V1 能为您带来哪些实际的提升——或者坦白说，哪些方面可能没有太大差别。”

2.8 常见顾客疑虑与应对方式

绝不要轻视顾客的任何疑虑。应先表示理解，再以平静、诚实的态度回应。以下四步法适用于几乎所有异议：

  1. 理解 — 表示您听懂了，并认同这个疑虑是合理的。
  2. 澄清 — 提出简短问题，了解疑虑背后真正的原因。
  3. 回应 — 以事实诚实回答，而不是施压。
  4. 确认 — 确认您的回答是否真正解除了顾客的疑虑。
疑虑背后真正的原因应对方式
价格（“为什么要RM9,980？”）对长期价值没有把握。先认同这确实是一项投资，再换算成产品使用期限内的每日成本，并与持续购买瓶装水的支出比较。
疑虑背后真正的原因应对方式
氢气消散较快担心功效不真实或无法持续。诚实说明氢水最好趁新鲜饮用，就像任何饮品一样，这是正常现象，并非产品缺陷。
保养／更换滤芯担心隐藏的麻烦或额外费用。清楚说明滤芯更换周期及包含内容，避免日后产生意外。
耗电量担心增加家庭日常用电成本。提供真实且偏低的耗电数据，并与其他小型家电做比较。
安装问题担心安装过程复杂或造成困扰。逐步说明安装流程，并告知由谁负责处理。
保修希望确保购买后不会被孤立无援。清楚说明保修范围、期限以及申请理赔的方式。
科学依据希望看到证据，而非营销话术。引用实际认证（NSF/ANSI 42、 SIRIM、KKM、SGS），避免夸大其代表的意义。
TRAINER NOTE 理解顾客的疑虑，并不等于承认产品有缺陷——它只是在告诉顾客“我听到了，我不会敷衍带过”。光是这一步，往往就能在您正式回答之前，化解掉不少异议。

2.9 黄金法则

顾客购买的并不是一台离子水机。他们购买的是信心、方便、更好的日常习惯，以及内心的安心感。Mizu V1 只是帮助他们达成这些目标的工具。

  • 先提问，再介绍产品。

Roadshow Sales SOP路演销售标准作业流程（SOP）

This chapter gives you a complete, step-by-step flow for handling a roadshow customer from the moment they walk up to your booth to the moment they walk away — either with a Mizu V1, or with a positive impression that brings them back. Every step builds on Chapter 2: you are still solving problems, not pushing a machine.

Objective: Give every agent a consistent, repeatable sales flow — with real sample conversations — so results don't depend on luck or who's working the booth that day.

The 8-Step Flow at a Glance

#StepGoal
1Greeting the CustomerGet a stranger to stop and feel welcome, not pressured.
2Ask Discovery QuestionsUnderstand their situation before saying anything about the product.
3Identify NeedsTurn what they told you into a clear problem worth solving.
4Introduce the ProductConnect the Mizu V1 directly to the need you just identified.
5Product DemonstrationShow, don't just tell — let the tests speak for themselves.
6Trial DrinkingLet the customer experience the water first-hand.
7Handle ObjectionsResolve concerns calmly using the Acknowledge– Clarify–Respond–Confirm method.
8Close the SaleMake the next step simple, low-pressure, and clear.
TRAINER NOTE This flow is sequential but not rigid. A customer may jump straight to an objection in Step 2, or ask to taste the water before you've finished demonstrating. Follow the customer's pace — the order is a guide, not a script to recite word-for-word.

STEP 1 Greeting the Customer

You have about 3–5 seconds to make someone feel welcome instead of “approached.” A generic “Hi, interested in a water filter?” invites a reflexive “no thanks.” Open instead with warmth, curiosity, or a light observation.

Do:

  • Smile first, speak second.
  • Use an open, low-pressure opening line.
  • Match their pace — if they're walking past slowly, a light comment works better than a hard stop.

Avoid:

  • “Can I help you?” — too easy to answer “no.”
  • Launching straight into product features.
  • Following too closely or blocking their path.

Sample Conversation — Greeting

AGENT: Hi there, good afternoon! Just taking a walk around today?

CUSTOMER: Yeah, just looking around.

AGENT: No worries at all, happy to have you stop by. We're actually doing a free water test today — takes less than two minutes, and you'll see exactly what's in the water you're drinking right now. Want to try it out, no obligation at all?

CUSTOMER: Sure, why not.

TRAINER NOTE Notice the agent never mentions “water machine” or price in the greeting. The free water test is a low-stakes, curiosity-driven invitation — it's far easier to say yes to than to a sales pitch.

STEP 2 Ask Discovery Questions

Once the customer has stopped, resist the urge to present. Use the discovery layers from Chapter 2 (Situation → Problem → Implication → Need-Payoff) to understand them first.

Sample Conversation — Discovery

AGENT: Before I show you anything, mind if I ask — what are you currently using for drinking water at home? Filter, bottled, or straight from the tap?

CUSTOMER: We just buy bottled water mostly.

AGENT: Got it. How many people are at home with you?

CUSTOMER: Four of us — my wife, two kids, and me.

AGENT: Nice. And roughly how much would you say you spend on bottled water a month, for the four of you?

CUSTOMER: Hmm, maybe RM150 to RM200?

AGENT: That adds up fast over a year. Has anyone in the family ever brought up concerns about tap water quality, or is it mainly about convenience?

CUSTOMER: A bit of both, honestly. My wife doesn't really trust the tap water for the kids.

TRAINER NOTE Notice the agent asked about household size and spending before touching the product at all. By the end of this exchange, the agent already knows the family's pain point (trust in tap water for the kids) and the financial angle (RM150–200/month) — both will be used later.

STEP 3 Identify Needs

This step is short but important: put the need into words out loud, so the customer hears it reflected back and confirms it themselves before you present anything.

Sample Conversation — Identifying the Need

AGENT: So if I'm understanding right — it's less about the cost of bottled water, and more about wanting your wife and kids to actually trust the water at home. Is that fair?

CUSTOMER: Yeah, that's pretty much it.

AGENT: That makes a lot of sense. Let me show you something that's helped a lot of families with exactly that concern.

TRAINER NOTE The agent summarised the need in the customer's own words and got a small “yes” before moving on. This single “yes” makes the customer far more receptive to the product introduction that follows.

STEP 4 Introduce the Product

Introduce the Mizu V1 as the answer to the specific need you just confirmed — not as a list of features. Keep the first introduction short; details come during the demonstration.

Sample Conversation — Introduction

AGENT: This is the Shimono Elite Mizu V1. In simple terms, it filters and ionises your tap water through four stages, so what comes out the other end isn't just filtered — it's tested clean, better-tasting, and safe enough for your kids to drink straight from the tap at home.

CUSTOMER: Is it hard to install?

AGENT: Not at all — it connects directly to your existing tap, and our team handles the installation. I'll walk you through that part too. But first, let me actually show you the difference rather than just tell you about it.

TRAINER NOTE The agent keeps the introduction to two sentences and steers straight into the demonstration. A long feature dump at this stage loses attention — proof, not explanation, is what moves a roadshow customer forward.

STEP 5 Product Demonstration

This is where trust gets built fastest. Use visible, simple tests — never claims that can't be shown. Common demo elements include the TDS/ORP meter reading, a hydrogen test, and a side-by- side comparison with tap or bottled water.

Sample Conversation — Demonstration

AGENT: Let's start with a quick reading. This is the tap water you'd normally drink — see this number on the meter? That's the ORP reading. Now let's run the same test on water from the Mizu V1.

CUSTOMER: Wow, that's a big difference.

AGENT: Exactly — that's the antioxidant potential I mentioned earlier. We're not just filtering out particles, we're changing the water's structure. Want to see the hydrogen test next?

CUSTOMER: Sure.

AGENT: Here's the reagent test — watch the colour change. This blue colour confirms hydrogen is present in the water. This is the same hydrogen-rich water your family would be drinking every day.

TRAINER NOTE Always let the customer watch the test happen — never just tell them the result. If a competitor's demo trick comes up (e.g. a visual hydrogen test that fades quickly), refer back to Chapter 2's guidance: compete on certified quality and verified readings, not on which visual trick looks more dramatic.

STEP 6 Trial Drinking

Letting the customer actually taste the water is one of the most persuasive moments in the entire flow — it turns an abstract claim into a felt, physical experience.

Sample Conversation — Trial Drinking

AGENT: Go ahead, try a cup of the filtered water — take your time with it.

CUSTOMER: It does taste smoother, actually. Lighter, somehow.

AGENT: That's what most people notice first — it's the same effect filtered, slightly alkaline water has. Now imagine your kids drinking this straight from the tap every morning, no more bottles to carry home.

TRAINER NOTE Avoid leading the customer with “Doesn't it taste amazing?” — let them describe the experience in their own words first, then connect it back to their stated need (their kids, their family).

STEP 7 Handle Objections

Use the four-step method from Chapter 2 — Acknowledge, Clarify, Respond, Confirm — for any concern that comes up here. Below is a worked example with the most common objection: price.

Sample Conversation — Handling a Price Objection

CUSTOMER: It does taste great, but RM9,980 is a lot of money.

AGENT: I completely understand — it's a real investment, and it's fair to think carefully about it. Can I ask, is it more the upfront amount that feels like a lot, or the overall price itself?

CUSTOMER: Mostly the upfront amount, I think.

AGENT: That makes sense. A few things that might help: first, we do have instalment options, so it doesn't have to be paid all at once. Second, if I break the RM9,980 down across the unit's typical lifespan, it actually works out to less than what you're currently spending on bottled water each month — and that's before counting the convenience of never running out. Does that change how the price feels?

CUSTOMER: Yeah, that actually helps a lot. Tell me more about the instalment option.

TRAINER NOTE Notice the agent never argued or got defensive about the price. They acknowledged it as a fair concern, clarified exactly what was bothering the customer, then responded with facts (instalments, cost-per-day) — and confirmed the objection was resolved before moving forward.

STEP 8 Close the Sale

Closing should feel like a natural next step, not a pressured moment. Offer a clear, simple choice rather than a vague “so, are you interested?”

Sample Conversation — Closing

AGENT: Based on everything we've talked about, I'd recommend going with the instalment plan since it's easier on your cash flow upfront. Would you like me to get the paperwork started today, or would you prefer I reserve today's roadshow pricing and follow up with you tomorrow once you've had a chance to discuss it with your wife?

CUSTOMER: Let's just start it today — I think this is the right one for us.

AGENT: Great choice — I'll get everything set up for you now. Let's start with a few details...

If the customer is not ready to commit:

AGENT: No problem at all — there's no pressure today. Let me give you my contact and a summary of what we discussed, so you and your wife can talk it over. I'll follow up in a couple of days to see how you're feeling about it.

TRAINER NOTE A customer who leaves with a positive experience — even without buying today — is still a win. Never let a soft close feel like rejection; log the follow-up and treat every contact as the start of a relationship, not a one-time transaction.

End of Chapter Summary

  • Greet warmly, with curiosity — never a hard sales opener.
  • Ask before you present; let discovery questions do the work.
  • Reflect the need back to the customer and get their agreement.
  • Introduce the product briefly, then move straight into proof.
  • Demonstrate visibly — let tests speak louder than claims.
  • Let the customer taste the water before describing it for them.
  • Handle every objection with Acknowledge → Clarify → Respond → Confirm.
  • Close with a clear, simple choice — and always leave the door open if they're not ready.

OBJECTION 2 Hydrogen Disappears? Doesn't That Mean It's Not Real?

This is a fair, scientifically accurate observation — and the honest answer is that it's normal, not a flaw.

Honest answer:

  • Dissolved hydrogen is a gas, and like any dissolved gas, it does reduce over time once water is exposed to air — this is true of every hydrogen-rich water on the market, not unique to any one brand.
  • The correct expectation to set is simple: hydrogen water is best consumed fresh, the same way you wouldn't expect a freshly opened soft drink to stay fizzy for hours.
  • Never claim the hydrogen 'stays' indefinitely — that claim will not survive a second question, and it will cost you the customer's trust.

Sample Script

CUSTOMER: I read somewhere that hydrogen in water disappears fast. So is it even worth anything?

AGENT: That's actually accurate, and I'd rather be upfront about it than oversell it. Hydrogen is a gas, so like any gas dissolved in water, it does reduce over time once it's exposed to air — that's true for hydrogen water in general, not just ours. The way to get the benefit is simply to drink it fresh, straight from the tap, rather than letting it sit around for hours. That's exactly how the Mizu V1 is meant to be used.

TRAINER NOTE This is the single most important objection to answer with full honesty. A customer who catches an agent overstating this will distrust everything else you say afterward.

代理: 这个说法没错——Coway 的 RO 系统确实能产出非常纯净、几乎不含矿物质的水，对一些家庭来说这正是他们最在意的。不过它的取舍是：RO 在去除杂质的同时，也会去除有益的矿物质，而且不会对水进行电解，所以不含氢气。如果您比较重视保留矿物质、碱性、富氢的水，并且希望一次性拥有而不是长期租赁，Mizu V1 正适合这个需求。如果您最关心的是绝对纯净、不含矿物质的水，Coway 也是一个合理的选择——我更希望您选到真正适合自己需求的产品。

OBJECTION 6 为什么不选 Kangen？

这是顾客最常问到的比较，因为 Kangen 是知名度最高的电解水机品牌。诚实的答案应该直接说明定价结构，而不是贬低产品本身。

诚实的答案：

  • Kangen（Enagic）机器只通过多层次直销（MLM）代理网络销售，价格通常视型号从约RM15,000 起，最高可超过 RM24,000——这个价格中有相当一部分是用来支付多层级代理的佣金，而不完全是技术本身的成本。
  • Kangen 机器做工扎实，使用的电解原理与市面上大多数电解水机相似，包括 Mizu V1——这项基础科学并非某一个品牌独有。
  • Mizu V1 采用直销模式，没有多层级分销加价，这正是它在使用相近电解原理的情况下，价格远低于 Kangen 的主要原因。
  • 绝不要说 Kangen“是假的”或“没用”——这并不准确，而且会损害您的可信度。诚实且站得住脚的论点是定价结构与性价比，而不是质疑产品的真实性。

示范对话

TRAINER NOTE 这是最容易被对 Kangen 代理的“技术更优越”说法有所了解的谨慎型顾客挑战的比较。请始终聚焦在定价结构等事实层面——不要被卷入您当下无法完全验证的技术争论中。

OBJECTION 7 5 块极板够用吗？

极板数量确实是一项真实的规格，但只是众多因素之一——诚实的答案既不会轻视这个问题，也不会夸大这个数字的意义。

诚实的答案：

  • 极板数量会影响电解可用的表面积，进而影响电解强度与 pH 范围——在其他条件相同的情况下，极板越多，pH 范围可能越宽，输出也可能越强。
  • 不过，极板数量本身并不能决定水质的好坏——极板材质与镀层品质、电源系统设计、电解前的过滤层级，以及相关认证，同样都很重要。
  • 对于一个家庭日常的饮用水需求（而非工业用途或大量清洁用水）而言，5 块设计精良的极板是真正足够的——市面上许多台式电解水机的极板数量从 4 到 9 块不等，视定位与价格档次而异。
  • 如果顾客最重视的是非饮用用途下尽可能宽的 pH 范围（例如强酸性清洁用水），应诚实告知极板数量较多的机器可能更适合这类特定需求。

示范对话

顾客： 5 块极板感觉有点少吧？我听说 Kangen 有 7 块或 8 块。

代理: 您问得很到位，极板数量确实是一个真实的考量因素。在其他条件相同的情况下，极板越多，pH范围可能越宽，输出也可能更强。但极板数量并不是唯一重要的因素——极板品质、电源系统，以及电解前的过滤层级同样起着很大的作用。对于日常家庭饮用水而言，5 块设计精良的极板是完全足够的——真正的关键在于您打算把这些水用在什么用途上。如果您特别需要非常强的酸性水来做工业级清洁，极板数量较多的机器可能更适合。但对于日常家庭饮用水来说，这已经绰绰有余。

OBJECTION 8 为什么要买这台机器？

这通常是最后一个总结性的问题——顾客想要的是一个清晰、诚实的理由来做决定，而不是把所有功能再重复一遍。

诚实的答案——回归到三个重点：

  • 它解决的是顾客在发掘阶段（第三章）告诉您的那个具体需求——这里不应该给出一个笼统的答案。
  • 它是一次性购买，品质经第三方认证，公开透明——不是持续缴费的租赁，也不是被多层分销加价的产品。
  • 它把过滤与电解功能合二为一，提供过滤、碱性、富氢的水，不需要承担竞争品牌长期租赁合约的负担，也不需要承担多层次直销品牌的分销加价。

示范对话

顾客： 好吧，这些比较我都明白了。但我到底为什么应该买这台机器？

代理: 回到您之前跟我说的——您希望家人能真正信任家里喝的水，又不想每月花上几百令吉买瓶装水。这正是这台机器要解决的问题：每天直接从水龙头喝到过滤、碱性、富氢的水。它是一次性购买，背后有真实的认证支持，不是需要持续缴费多年的租赁，价格里也没有分销网络的加价。说到底，这是最直接能解决您今天来这里所关心的问题的方式。

  • 与竞争对手公平比较，立足于事实（价格结构、技术类型、合约条款）——绝不贬低对方。
  • 对真实存在的局限性（氢气消散、研究仍在进行中）保持坦诚——可信度比任何一次成交都更持久。

Closing Techniques促成成交的技巧

Closing isn't a separate skill bolted onto the end of a conversation — it's the natural result of everything in Chapters 2, 3, 5, and 6 done well. A customer who feels understood, who has seen real proof, and whose objections were honestly resolved, rarely needs to be “closed” in a pushy sense. This chapter teaches you how to ask for the sale clearly, explain price and payments with confidence, and turn every roadshow conversation — sale or not — into long-term value through referrals and follow-up.

Objective: Give every agent a confident, low-pressure way to ask for the sale, explain value and price, set up payment plans, generate referrals, and follow up — with ready-to-use scripts for each.

7.1 How to Ask for the Sale

Many agents lose sales not because the customer said no, but because the agent never actually asked. Asking clearly is a sign of confidence, not pressure — as long as it follows genuine discovery, demonstration, and objection handling.

Three reliable ways to ask:

  • The Choice Close — offer two acceptable paths forward, rather than a yes/no question.
  • The Summary Close — briefly recap the need and the proof, then ask directly.
  • The Assumptive Close — once the customer has clearly responded well to the demo and trial, move into the next steps naturally, as though the decision is already made, while still leaving room to pause.

Sample Script — Choice Close

AGENT: Would you like to go ahead with the full payment today, or would the instalment plan work better for your family?

Sample Script — Summary Close

AGENT: So to recap — you wanted your family to actually trust the water at home, you've seen the test results yourself, and you liked the taste. Shall we get you started today?

Sample Script — Assumptive Close

AGENT: Great, let's get your details down so I can have everything ready for installation this week.

TRAINER NOTE Never use the Assumptive Close if the customer hasn't clearly shown buying signals (leaning in, asking “how do I...” questions, nodding at the price explanation). Used too early, it feels pushy; used at the right moment, it feels natural.

7.2 How to Create Value

Value isn't a list of features — it's the gap between what the customer has now and what they get with the Mizu V1, expressed in terms that matter to them personally.

A simple way to build value before the close:

  1. Restate their need in their own words (“You mentioned your wife doesn't trust the tap water for the kids.”)
  2. Connect that need directly to what they experienced in the demo and trial (“You saw the ORP difference yourself, and you said the water tasted lighter.”)
  3. Frame the cost against what they're already spending, not against the sticker price alone (“That's less than what you're currently spending on bottled water each month.”)

Sample Script — Building Value

AGENT: Let's put this together — you wanted your kids drinking water your wife actually trusts, you saw the test results yourself, and the taste difference was real, not just a sales pitch. At less than what you're currently spending on bottled water, this isn't really an added cost — it's replacing a cost you already have, with something better.

7.3 Price Explanation

Refer back to Chapter 5's full answer to “Why RM9,980?” for the underlying reasoning. At the closing stage, the goal is to present the price clearly and confidently — never apologetically, and never buried in a long list of features.

A clean price explanation has three parts:

  1. State the price plainly, with no hesitation in your voice.
  2. Immediately translate it into cost-per-day or cost-per-month context.
  3. Mention what's included (certifications, installation, warranty) so the number doesn't stand alone.

Sample Script — Price Explanation

AGENT: The Mizu V1 is RM9,980. Spread across its typical lifespan, that works out to just a few ringgit a day — less than what most families spend on bottled water monthly. That price includes installation, the certified filtration system, and our warranty coverage, so there's nothing extra to add on afterward.

7.4 Monthly Payment Explanation

Some customers can comfortably pay RM9,980 outright; many would simply prefer not to, even if they could. Presenting the instalment option as a deliberate choice — not a fallback for customers who “can't afford it” — keeps the conversation respectful.

Key points to cover:

  • Instalment plans via Payex allow the cost to be spread over a fixed period (12, 35, or 60 months) rather than paid in one lump sum, with EzBeli available as a split-card option for customers whose single card limit can't cover the full amount.
  • Be transparent about the total cost under instalment versus outright payment — some plans carry additional interest or fees, and customers deserve to know this upfront.
  • Confirm eligibility requirements honestly rather than assuming approval — never guarantee approval on behalf of Payex or EzBeli.

Sample Script — Monthly Payment Explanation

AGENT: If you'd rather not pay the full RM9,980 at once, we do offer instalment plans through Payex — the total comes to RM10,980 spread over 12, 35, or 60 months, which works out to as low as RM183 a month on the 60-month plan. Approval depends on Payex's own assessment of your application, but it's a comfortable option for a lot of our customers. Want me to check what your monthly amount would look like?

TRAINER NOTE Never promise a specific monthly figure before checking with Payex directly — quote the published 12/35/60-month figures (RM915 / RM314 / RM183 per month) and confirm the exact figure with Payex at the time of application, since the 35-month figure can vary slightly due to rounding.

7.5 Referral Techniques

A satisfied customer is one of your most valuable assets — but referrals rarely happen unless you ask clearly and make it easy.

Best time to ask:

  • Right after the customer has confirmed the purchase, while their enthusiasm is highest.
  • During a positive follow-up call, once they've used the Mizu V1 for a week or two and can speak to it from real experience.

A lot of our customers come through people they trust rather than another roadshow visit — if you know one or two friends or family members who've mentioned wanting better water at home, I'd really appreciate an introduction. I'll personally make sure they're well taken care of.

7.6 Follow-Up After Roadshow

The roadshow conversation is the beginning of the relationship, not the end of it — whether or not the customer purchased on the spot.

For customers who purchased:

  • Follow up within the first week to check the unit is working well and answer any early questions.

8.1 了解技术（Q1–15）

QuestionAnswer
Q1. 什么是氢水？含有溶解氢气（H₂）分子的水。Mizu V1 通过电解产生氢气，使过滤后的水达到可测量的浓度（最高 1,600 ppb）。
Q2. 什么是 ORP？氧化还原电位——以毫伏（mV）衡量物质获得或失去电子的倾向。负值的 ORP 代表抗氧化潜力；Mizu V1 产出的水 ORP 为 −200 至 −400 mV。
Q3. 什么是 pH？一个 0 到 14 的标尺，用来衡量水的酸碱程度。7 为中性；低于 7 为酸性；高于 7 为碱性。Mizu V1 可在 8 种模式下调整 pH，范围为 4.0 至 10.5。
Q4. 为什么要喝碱性水？许多人偏好碱性水（pH 高于 7）作为日常饮用水，因其口感与矿物质含量较好，适合日常补水与料理使用。
Q5. 为什么需要酸性水？微酸性水（较低 pH 模式）通常用于外部用途，例如护肤或清洁，而不是用来饮用。
Q6. 氢气会消散吗？会——溶解的氢气一旦接触空气，浓度会随时间降低，就像汽水的气泡一样。这是所有富氢水的共同特性，并非某个品牌独有。建议趁新鲜饮用以获得最佳效果。
Q7. 什么是自由基？一种带有未成对电子、不稳定的分子，会与体内其他分子发生剧烈反应，是新陈代谢过程中正常产生的副产物。
Q8. 自由基会如何影响身体？过量时，自由基可能损害细胞、蛋白质与 DNA——这个过程称为氧化压力，研究人员认为它与衰老及多种健康状况有关。身体也会自然产生抗氧化物来帮助维持这种平衡。
Q9. 什么是抗氧化物？一种能够中和自由基的分子，它会提供一个电子而不让自己变得不稳定，从而帮助保护细胞免受氧化损伤。
Q10. 氢水是抗氧化物吗？氢分子被研究为一种选择性抗氧化物。关于其更广泛健康效果的研究仍在进行中，尚无最终结论——我们诚实地把它呈现为一项仍在发展中的科学领域，而不是保证有效的疗法。
Q11. 电解的作用是什么？通过电流流经带电极板，将水分解为碱性与酸性两路水流，同时产生溶解氢气。
Q12. 为什么 Mizu V1 使用 5 块极板？5 块设计精良的极板，足以满足家庭日常饮用水的需求。极板品质、电源设计与过滤系统，与极板数量同样重要。
QuestionAnswer
Q13. 什么是 TDS？总溶解固体——衡量水中溶解矿物质与微粒总量的指标，通常以 ppm 为单位。
Q14. ppb 是什么意思？十亿分之一——用于衡量水中溶解氢气浓度的单位。
Q15. 四阶段过滤分别是什么？PPF（沉淀物预过滤）、CTO 活性炭块、Carbonite Core 第一阶段（陶瓷），以及 Carbonite Core 第二阶段（UF 中空纤维）——每一阶段针对电解前不同的杂质进行处理。

8.6 竞争品牌比较问题（Q64–71）

QuestionAnswer
Q64. 为什么不选 Kangen？Kangen 通过多层次直销代理网络销售，价格通常远高于Mizu V1，其中相当一部分是用来支付代理佣金，而不完全是核心技术本身的成本。
Q65. 为什么不选 Cuckoo？Cuckoo 是一台使用纳滤技术的净水机；它不进行电解，也不产生富氢水，且通常以多年期租赁合约销售，而不是一次性购买。
Q66. 为什么不选 Coway？Coway 主要使用逆渗透过滤技术，能产出非常纯净但不含矿物质的水；它同样不进行电解或产生富氢水，且采用租赁模式。
Q67. 与其他电解水机相比，5 块极板够用吗？对于家庭日常饮用水而言，是足够的——极板品质、电源设计与过滤层级，与极板数量本身同样重要。极板数量较多的机器可能更适合特定的非饮用用途。
Q68. Mizu V1 与瓶装水有什么不同？它能在家中随时提供过滤、碱性、富氢的水，省去持续购买与搬运瓶装水的费用与不便。
Q69. 这是多层次直销（MLM）产品吗？不是——Shimono Elite 的精英圈计划是符合马来西亚《2021 年直销法令》的双层直销结构，不是传统的MLM 模式。
Q70. 如果两者都能电解水，为什么 Mizu V1 比Kangen 便宜？Mizu V1 采用直销模式，没有多层级代理佣金结构，这正是在使用相近电解原理的情况下，价格存在差异的主要原因。
Q71. Mizu V1 与［其他品牌］相比哪个更好？这取决于您的需求重点：电解功能与一次性拥有，更适合选择 Mizu V1；超纯净不含矿物质的水或低维护的租赁模式，则更适合选择以 RO 为核心的品牌。我们会帮助顾客选择真正符合自身需求的产品。
